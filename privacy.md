# Expense Manager — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 9, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Expense Manager is a personal finance application that helps users record expenses, manage budgets, and organize related financial information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

## 2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Expense Manager may handle:

- **Account information:** Your sign-in email address and information associated with your account.
- **Financial information:** Transactions, budgets, debts, categories, preferences, and other information you enter into the application.
- **Google Drive backup information:** Information and files needed to perform a backup or restore when you choose to use that feature.
- **Support communications:** Information you voluntarily provide when contacting support.

Please avoid entering information you do not want processed by the application.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Authenticate your account and provide application features.
- Store and synchronize your financial records.
- Perform backups and restore data when you request these operations.
- Troubleshoot technical problems and respond to support requests.
- Maintain the security and reliability of the application.

## 4. Storage and Third-Party Services

Expense Manager uses Firebase Authentication for account authentication and Cloud Firestore for storing application data. Google services are used to authorize and perform Google Drive backup and restore operations when you choose to use those features.

These service providers process information according to their own applicable terms and privacy policies. The application does not intend to sell personal information or use Google Drive data for advertising.

## 5. Google Drive Data

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the application requests authorization to support its backup and restore functionality.

Google account information and Drive data are used only as needed to provide the features you authorize, consistent with this policy and the permissions requested by the application. The application should not access unrelated Drive content or use Google data for unrelated purposes.

You can revoke the application's access through your Google Account security settings. Revoking access may prevent future backups or restores until you authorize access again.

## 6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described in this policy to operate the application. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or to address legitimate security concerns.

Information you choose to share independently with another person or service is subject to your choices and that service's policies.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's features and meet applicable operational or legal requirements.

To ask questions about your information or request deletion of data associated with your account, contact **ish2000inth@gmail.com**. Please contact us using the email address associated with your account where possible so we can verify the request.

Some information may remain in backups for a limited period or where retention is required by law.

## 8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to help protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their account credentials and secure their devices.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Expense Manager is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
